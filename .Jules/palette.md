## 2026-07-25 - Modal Close Button Accessibility\n**Learning:** Found multiple modal close buttons represented by `&times;` or icon-only SVGs that were missing `aria-label`s for screen reader announcements in standard modal patterns.\n**Action:** When adding modals or close buttons, always proactively include an `aria-label="Close modal"` to ensure the `&times;` character is not misread by screen readers.
